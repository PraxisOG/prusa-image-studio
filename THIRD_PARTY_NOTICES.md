## Color prediction

`palette.py` adapts the v7 `prusa-fdm-mixer` color prediction from Prusa Research's ColorMix Shading application. Its original copyright and MIT license are reproduced in `licenses/prusa-fdm-mixer.txt`. The adaptation is Python code for five physical filaments and equal two-filament blends; the base filament hex values are unmeasured estimates.

Upstream source: https://github.com/prusa3d/ColorMix-Shading/tree/main/src/lib/prusa-fdm-mixer

## Slicer format and stock profiles

The native project format was checked against [PrusaSlicer 3.0.0-alpha13](https://github.com/prusa3d/PrusaSlicer/tree/version_3.0.0-alpha13), especially `src/slic3r-shared/src/Slic3r/Biz/Format/3mf/PrusaFile.cpp` and `src/slic3r-shared/src/Slic3r/Biz/Format/VirtualExtruder.cpp`.

The bundled `assets/xl_sunlu_petg.3mf` was generated by that version's CLI from its stock XL five-tool, standard-flow 0.4 mm, 0.10 mm layer, and Sunlu PETG profiles, then stripped of temporary source paths. Prusa Research retains the rights to its configuration and G-code material. PrusaSlicer is distributed under [GNU AGPL v3](https://github.com/prusa3d/PrusaSlicer/blob/version_3.0.0-alpha13/LICENSE), reproduced in this project's `LICENSE`. The source and instructions for regenerating the template are included in `project.py` and the `refresh-template` CLI command. No slicer executable is bundled.
